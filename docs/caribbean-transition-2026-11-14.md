# Caribbean transition evidence, 14 November 2026

Dataset `2026-11-14.1` adds five dated government-source observations. It does
not re-audit tariffs, household eligibility or destination travel rules. Their
claim revisions and review dates remain unchanged. All prior release bytes are
retained.

- St Kitts and Nevis: the Prime Minister’s 13 November address announces a
  negotiated wind-down. Applications continue until an agreed final receipt
  date is announced. Intake is `published_with_limits`, not closed.
- Saint Lucia: the government’s briefing dated 9 November confirms a response
  was sent to the EU. Its contents were withheld; no phase-out decision is inferred.
- Dominica: the 28 October government statement describes the EU request,
  transition/support talks and planned further communication by 12 November.
  It does not establish the outcome after that communication date.
- Antigua and Barbuda: the government-published Independence address dated
  1 November confirms the EU June 2028 request and ongoing negotiations. The
  new observation does not establish a final transition agreement.
- Grenada: the Ministry of Finance’s 2026 mid-year report, section 12.1.2,
  identifies EU phase-out pressure and revenue risk. A newer reported political
  position is not promoted without its original statement.

The two original recordings were reviewed through timestamped automatic
transcripts on the government or national broadcaster’s channel. Transcription
errors in country names were normalized; no recording or transcript is
redistributed. Source records contain original links and precise locators.

These observations describe announcements. Their `effectiveFrom` and change
`effectiveAt` stay null. No application deadline or actual visa suspension is
invented, and no indefinite travel guarantee is inferred from statements about
existing citizens. This is original factual writing contributed under the
repository’s compilation licence; upstream publications retain their rights.
