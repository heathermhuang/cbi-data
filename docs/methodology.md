# Methodology

Canonical JSON files are human-readable original CBI research. They preserve
individual claim revisions, typed values, evidence locators, review dates,
conditions and limitations. A partial verification state means the dated source
basis supports only that stated scope. No numeric confidence score is assigned.
A retrieval observation does not confirm a legal interpretation or refresh a review.
Known, unknown, not-stated and not-applicable are separate value states.

Programme legal mechanism, published intake, decision and passport issuance are
separate. A published application page is not proof of a completed issuance.
A consular advertisement is identified as such, not promoted to legislation.
Conflicting official prices remain disputed with both references. Legal basis,
financial-resources, screening and other incompletely researched groups have
named gaps even where a neighbouring field is known. No source excerpts or
PDF corpus are redistributed; normalized factual values are original compilation.

Costs use checked decimal strings and BigInt minor units. Capital and charges
remain separate line items with currency, applicant basis, quantity, payment stage,
recoverability and frequency. The two fixed household scenarios are conditional
illustrations, not a determination that an individual or dependant is accepted.
Unknown charges prevent an all-in total. Different currencies are never converted.
Expired, future-effective and disputed items are excluded from current subtotals.
Recorded values remain inspectable with expired/requiresRecheck flags and must
not be used as current values. Filing cutoffs with unknown timezone use the
conservative earliest day boundary.

Travel covers ordinary national passports after issuance and short visits only.
A country/regime is not its territories. Schengen nationality policy does not
establish every member's document recognition. Canada's conditional air eTA
requires explicit transport and qualifying-visa context; unknown context stays
conditional. US visa-waiver membership, issuance restrictions, entry restrictions
and personal exceptions remain distinct. No application/approval guarantee is made.

Dataset release identifies what the compilation recorded; it is not historical
legal reconstruction at arbitrary dates. Generated time is fixed release metadata;
evaluation time computes overdue and expiry labels. Source health is dated and
separate. A reviewer must assess a changed source before accepting any claim change.
