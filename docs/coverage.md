# Coverage and limits

Release 2026-10-25.2: Antigua and Barbuda (ATG), Dominica (DMA), Grenada (GRD),
Saint Kitts and Nevis (KNA), Saint Lucia (LCA), Türkiye (TUR), Egypt (EGY),
Vanuatu (VUT), Nauru (NRU), São Tomé and Príncipe (STP), El Salvador (SLV).
Legacy IDs are aliases, not additional programmes. Each option has its own ID.

Each of sixteen required field groups per programme has sourced partial claims
or a named gap. Partial does not mean fully verified. `/api/v1/coverage` and the
immutable manifest contain exact generated counts, review-due counts and scope.
No field group is labelled supported merely because a source URL exists.
Two scenarios: single adult; principal, spouse and two children below twelve.
Family dependency definitions and missing government charges stay unresolved
unless explicitly documented; comprehensive totals are null for this release.

All 44 travel slots have explicit qualified outcomes and dated claim references.
Missing territories, special/noncitizen documents, work purposes and individual
status exceptions return not_covered or required context, never an invented visa.
General global mobility counts and paid provider observations are excluded.

Selected investor residence is a subsequent gated milestone. Broader travel
expansion needs its own source/rights inventory and a maintainable review backlog.
Maintainer tests demonstrate technical availability, not independent adoption.
